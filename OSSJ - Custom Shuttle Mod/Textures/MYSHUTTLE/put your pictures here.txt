Rename this folder to your shuttle's name, then put your pictures in it, like:

    RedRocket_north.png     seen from above, nose pointing up
    RedRocket_east.png      seen from above, nose pointing right
    RedRocket_south.png     seen from above, nose pointing down

West is mirrored from east automatically.

Then open Defs\MyShuttle.xml and follow the note in the LOOK section.
You can delete this file.
